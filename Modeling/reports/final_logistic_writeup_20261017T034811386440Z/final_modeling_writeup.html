Executive summary

The selected approach is an untuned logistic-regression pipeline using the approved core predictors and training-only treatment controls. In exploratory rolling validation, overall recall at 1,000 was 80.60%, precision was 3.60%, and lift was 26.11 times the pooled base rate.

The primary evidence is the FY2022–FY2024 rolling assessment, not the FY2025 influx-period check. The saved final pipeline was subsequently refitted on FY2021–FY2025 using 160,919 donor-year rows. Its standalone artifact passed fresh-process reload and scoring-control tests. That final refit has no independent post-refit performance estimate.

The saved model has now scored 38,219 eligible FY2026 prospects. A preliminary overall person-level top-1,000 file contains 1000 rows, allocating 1,000 places. No new model fitting or FY2025 evaluation occurred during scoring.

The main operational trade-off is clear: logistic improves overall converter capture primarily by allocating more places to eligible former-major donors. An improvement for never-major outreach was not established. Scores are not validated individual conversion probabilities.

Objective, population, and features

The target is converted_1yr: major status in the next fiscal year, not first-ever major status. Eligible donors gave positively in fiscal year t and were not major in that same year. Former-major donors remain eligible; lapsed donors are a separate reporting population.

The group definitions recognize positive direct giving and assigned soft credits, with the documented spouse-combined major-status rule. Prospect records remain person-level even where labels reflect combined spouse giving. Household deduplication is a separate operational decision.

The final model uses the approved core feature list below. Identifiers, outcome amounts, evaluation weights, and future information are not supplied as predictors.

Numeric and binary inputs use training-median imputation and standardization. gender and giving_change_band use constant Unknown imputation and one-hot encoding, with unseen levels ignored. Preprocessing was fitted within each rolling training fold and refitted on the approved full training period for the final artifact.

Logistic regression uses C=1, L2 regularization, the lbfgs solver, and a 1,000-iteration convergence cap. No hyperparameter tuning, class weighting, resampling, or recalibration was performed. Actual contacted_t and heldout_t flags are used in training; both are switched off for all scoring. Actual exposure flags remain separate for evaluation.

Saved split membership was used. Earlier training years can contain donors observed in later assessment years. No inner tuning was performed; any future inner tuning must group by couple_id. All learned preprocessing was restricted to training data.

Primary selection used recall at 1,000 prospects per assessment year. Cutoff ties received fractional allocation. Overall and never-major evaluations each use their own 1,000-prospect list; they must not be confused with subgroup capture within one overall list.

Recall, precision, and lift are pooled over annual lists. AP and ROC AUC below are means of annual metrics. Accuracy at 0.5 uses probability-threshold classifications; list accuracy treats list inclusion as positive with fractional ties. Ranking-only baselines have no probability-threshold accuracy. Accuracy is secondary because conversion is rare.

Intervals use 1,000 couple-clustered bootstrap replicates, recalculating list cutoffs. They are conditional on fitted predictions and do not represent the entire model-selection uncertainty. Prior full-data EDA occurred: these are exploratory validation results, not an untouched-test claim.

FY2025 disruption check: separate secondary evidence

FY2025 was excluded from model selection and primary rolling validation. Its next-year outcomes fall in FY2026, July 1, 2025–June 30, 2026, the influx period identified in the EDA. After logistic was selected, a frozen pipeline trained on FY2021–FY2024 was evaluated once on FY2025. FY2025 was then included in the approved final refit. It was not permanently left out of training.

Development predictions overpredicted conversion on average, particularly for no-contact populations; the FY2025 check underpredicted conversion on average. Better ranking or a lower Brier score does not establish good calibration at the top of the prospect list. Overall observed outcomes also include actual contact, whereas model inputs neutralize treatment controls; weighted no-contact evaluation more closely matches the intended scoring setting.

No calibrator was fitted. Including FY2025 in the final refit does not demonstrate that calibration is repaired. Do not interpret sums of FY2026 scores as a validated forecast of conversions or scores as estimates of outreach effects.

The source builder was reconstructed with reconciled counts, not guaranteed identical original feature values. Age was reconstructed from a later snapshot, and legacy records contribute to giving history and former-major flags. These provenance limitations should remain visible. Person-level scoring is not equivalent to household-level outreach.

Final refit and FY2026 scoring deliverables

The selected specification was refitted on FY2021–FY2025 without tuning or recalibration. The saved artifact includes learned preprocessing, package versions, hashes, and a control-safe inference wrapper. Reload tests used synthetic inputs and did not repeat FY2025 evaluation.

For this release, the artifact and input hashes were verified and the saved model—not a newly fitted model—scored FY2026. Actual exposure flags are preserved in the export, but both model controls were neutralized. Current scoring uses equal person-level allocation weights, not the historical no-contact evaluation weights.
